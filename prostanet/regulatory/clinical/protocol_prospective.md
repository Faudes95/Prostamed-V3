## 1. Title

**Prospective Multi-Center Validation of ProstaMed Clinical Decision Engine
in Latin American Patients with Prostate Cancer (PROSTAMED-LAT)**

## 2. Background

ProstaMed is a 510(k) Class II SaMD providing clinical decision support for
prostate cancer management. While its 89 pivotal gates and 47 trial
eligibility evaluators are validated against NCCN 5.2026 + EAU 2026 +
international literature, **prospective validation in Mexican / Latin
American population is lacking**.

This study evaluates whether ProstaMed recommendations correlate with
expert-panel consensus + actual clinical outcomes.

## 3. Hypothesis

ProstaMed recommendations (treatment selection, risk stratification, transition
proposals) demonstrate:
- **Sensitivity** ≥ 0.85 vs expert panel consensus
- **Specificity** ≥ 0.80 vs expert panel consensus
- **Concordance** with actual treatment outcome (24-mo PFS) ≥ 0.75

## 4. Study Design

- **Type**: Prospective, multi-center, observational
- **Sites**: 5 oncology referral centers (Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey + 2 TBD)
- **Sample size**: N = 500 patients
- **Duration**: 12-month enrollment + 12-month follow-up (24 months total)
- **Inclusion**: Adult males ≥18, biopsy-confirmed or suspected prostate adenocarcinoma
- **Exclusion**: Pediatric, non-prostate primary, life expectancy <6 months at enrollment

## 5. Endpoints

### Primary
- Concordance ProstaMed recommendation vs expert panel consensus

### Secondary
- 24-month progression-free survival (PFS) by ProstaMed risk stratum
- Trial eligibility precision (47 trials)
- Transition proposal acceptance rate
- Override rate per gate (with reasons)

## 6. Sample Size Justification

For sensitivity = 0.85 ± 0.05 (95% CI), with disease prevalence ~50% in
suspected cohort, N = 500 provides 90% power.
